## Tip of the day [Wednesday, July 22, 2026]

### Layer network defenses, don’t just open ports

Start by mapping the exact data flows your workload needs, including who initiates each connection, which ports and protocols are required, and which components need public versus internal access. Segment the environment into network layers based on sensitivity and access requirements, so databases and other private services stay isolated from internet-facing endpoints. Use security groups as the first line of control, then add route tables and firewalls to enforce finer-grained rules within and across VPCs. For public web traffic, place inspection points such as AWS WAF in front of endpoints, and consider AWS Network Firewall or Route 53 Resolver DNS Firewall for deeper filtering. Where possible, prefer private connectivity services like AWS PrivateLink to reduce exposure of internal services to the internet. Automate and centrally manage these controls with tools like AWS Firewall Manager so protections stay consistent as your network changes.

**Why it matters:** Network protection is strongest when it uses multiple layers of defense instead of relying on a single perimeter rule set. Clear segmentation and inspection reduce the blast radius of misconfigurations and make it much harder for unwanted traffic to reach sensitive systems.
