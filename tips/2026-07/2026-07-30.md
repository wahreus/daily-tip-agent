## Tip of the day [Thursday, July 30, 2026]

### Pick the region that balances sustainability with your workload needs

When selecting an AWS Region, start with your non-negotiable requirements such as compliance, data sovereignty, feature availability, latency, and cost. Then compare the remaining candidate Regions against your sustainability goals, since Region choice directly affects performance, cost, and carbon footprint. Use the AWS Regional Services List to verify that the services and features you need are available in each shortlist candidate. If you track emissions, align the Region choice with the method you use to measure carbon output so your reporting stays consistent year to year. Avoid choosing a Region just because it is closest to your team or because all workloads are already concentrated in one place. A more deliberate Region strategy can improve both operational efficiency and sustainability outcomes.

**Why it matters:** Region selection is one of the simplest architecture decisions with a measurable sustainability impact. Making it explicit helps reduce avoidable emissions without sacrificing business requirements, and it prevents hidden tradeoffs in performance or compliance later.
