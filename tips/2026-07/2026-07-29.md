## Tip of the day [Wednesday, July 29, 2026]

### Track sustainability with the same discipline as performance

Define a small set of sustainability metrics for each workload, such as resource utilization, instance runtime, and carbon accounting boundaries, and review them alongside cost and performance dashboards. Use CloudWatch and related monitoring data to identify underutilized compute, memory, disk, or network capacity so you can right-size resources instead of running oversized infrastructure. Where possible, consolidate metrics into a single platform with retention policies that fit your operational needs. Tie these measurements back to organizational sustainability goals, including carbon reduction and resource optimization, so engineering decisions have a clear target. If your team runs batch jobs, consider moving heavy operations to highly utilized scalable infrastructure rather than underused single-user systems. This turns sustainability from a vague aspiration into an engineering feedback loop.

**Why it matters:** Sustainability improves when it is measured, not assumed. Clear metrics help teams find waste, justify right-sizing, and show progress toward carbon and resource goals.
