## Tip of the day [Tuesday, July 28, 2026]

### Replace low-utilization components with managed services

Review your workload for components that can move from self-managed infrastructure to AWS managed services such as Amazon RDS, Amazon Redshift, Amazon ElastiCache, or Amazon Athena. Managed services can improve sustainability by helping keep underlying hardware at higher utilization and by reducing the operational overhead of patching, upgrading, and routine maintenance. That usually lets your team spend less time on undifferentiated work and more time on simplifications, right-sizing, and innovation. When you make the switch, inventory the workload first and identify the parts that are most expensive to operate or most likely to sit underused. Where possible, pair the migration with capacity tuning so the managed service runs close to actual demand rather than with excess headroom.

**Why it matters:** Higher utilization generally means less wasted compute and better resource efficiency, which supports both sustainability and cost optimization. It also reduces the human effort needed to run the service, freeing engineers to improve the parts of the system that matter most.
