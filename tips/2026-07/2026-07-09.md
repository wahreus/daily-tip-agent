## Tip of the day [Thursday, July 9, 2026]

### Monitor quotas before they become incidents

Treat service quotas as a capacity signal, not just a documentation detail. Start by identifying the default limits for the AWS services your workload depends on, especially for scaling paths and APIs that can throttle under load. Set automated alerts when usage approaches your defined thresholds so you have time to act before a quota breach affects customers. Use AWS Service Quotas to look up current limits and request increases early, since some approvals are fast but larger requests can take time. For quotas that cannot be adjusted, design the workload to stay within the hard limit or to degrade gracefully when it is reached.

**Why it matters:** Quota-related failures often appear as sudden throttling, deployment failures, or partial outages even when the application code is healthy. Proactive monitoring and early quota planning reduce the risk of service disruption during traffic spikes, growth, or recovery events.
