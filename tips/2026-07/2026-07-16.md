## Tip of the day [Thu, July 16, 2026]

### Make cost visibility actionable with budgets, tags, and regular reviews

Set up cost visibility so you can see spend by account, service, and workload, not just as a monthly bill. Use consistent tags or cost categories to attribute resources to teams and applications, then create budgets at both the account and workload level. Pair AWS Cost Explorer with AWS Budgets so you can filter usage, track trends, and get alerts before costs exceed thresholds. For shared infrastructure, make sure your allocation approach reflects workload metrics or business outcomes so teams understand what is driving spend. Review these reports regularly with engineering and finance so you can spot negative trends early and turn cost data into concrete actions.

**Why it matters:** Without clear cost and usage visibility, teams can’t tell which services, workloads, or shared resources are driving spend. Regular attribution and budget tracking improve accountability, speed up corrections, and make cost optimization a continuous operational practice.
