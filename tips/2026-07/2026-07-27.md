## Tip of the day [Monday, July 27, 2026]

### Measure sustainability with utilization and emission KPIs

Track sustainability as a set of operational metrics, not just a quarterly report. Start by defining a small group of KPIs that connect workload output to resource use, such as CPU, memory, disk, and network utilization alongside emissions or energy estimates per unit of work. Collect those metrics in one place, and apply retention policies so the data stays useful without creating unnecessary overhead. Review the metrics regularly to spot underutilized resources, and use the results to right-size instances or shift to managed services where appropriate. Where possible, align the metrics with your organization's carbon accounting boundaries so your engineering teams and sustainability stakeholders are working from the same numbers.

**Why it matters:** Sustainability goals are easier to act on when they are measurable at the workload level. Good metrics help you identify waste, compare efficiency over time, and prove whether changes like right-sizing or managed services are actually reducing impact.
