## Tip of the day [Wednesday, July 15, 2026]

### Centralize security logs in a hardened archive account

Capture security logs and findings from workloads and AWS services into standardized locations, ideally a dedicated Log Archive account. This gives your security team a single place to correlate events from sources like CloudTrail, WAF, Elastic Load Balancing, Route 53, and other service logs. Use cross-account permissions so workloads can publish logs without broad access to the archive. Keep the archive isolated so a compromised workload cannot easily tamper with historical evidence. For best results, pair the centralized store with a SIEM or detection pipeline that can query and analyze the data quickly.

**Why it matters:** Centralized logging makes investigations faster because analysts can search one trusted location instead of chasing scattered logs across accounts and services. It also improves resilience against tampering and supports compliance by preserving logs for retention and audit purposes.
