## Tip of the day [Tuesday, July 14, 2026]

### Prefer managed services when they reduce operational overhead

When evaluating a workload for cost reduction, look beyond instance pricing and compare the total cost of ownership of self-managed components versus AWS managed services. Managed services can remove patching, upgrades, backups, and other administrative tasks, which lowers operational effort and often reduces license or maintenance costs. They can also improve price efficiency by operating at cloud scale, sometimes delivering a lower cost per transaction than running the same capability on virtual machines. Review your workload inventory and identify components such as databases, caching, search, or analytics engines that could be replaced with managed offerings like Amazon RDS, Amazon ElastiCache, Amazon OpenSearch Service, or Amazon Athena. Use managed services especially where your team is spending time on upkeep instead of building features or retiring technical debt. Make sure the service choice still fits the workload’s technical needs, but treat managed services as a first-line cost optimization option rather than an afterthought.

**Why it matters:** The biggest savings often come from reducing the hidden costs of operations, not just shrinking infrastructure spend. By shifting routine maintenance to AWS, your team can spend more time on innovation while the platform handles much of the undifferentiated heavy lifting.
