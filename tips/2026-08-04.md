## Tip of the day [Tue, August 4, 2026]

### Pick the architecture that fits the workload, then prove it with data

Architecture selection should start with the workload’s business needs, performance targets, and operating constraints, not with a favorite pattern. In practice, the best solution often combines multiple approaches, so it is worth considering whether parts of the workload should use different architectures or resource types. Use cloud-provider guidance, reference architectures, and internal experience to narrow the options, then validate the choice with benchmarking or small experiments. Factor cost and trade-offs into the decision early, including how much complexity your team can support and how the design will evolve over time. Revisit the choice as the workload matures, because what is right for a fast-moving launch can differ from what is right for a system that must scale efficiently.

**Why it matters:** A disciplined selection process helps you avoid overengineering, unnecessary rework, and architecture changes made without justification. It also gives you a defensible way to balance performance, cost, and operational simplicity as requirements change.
