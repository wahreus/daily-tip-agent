## Tip of the day [Friday, September 25, 2026]

### Predefine escalation paths before the pager rings

For event response, make escalation a built-in part of your incident process rather than an ad hoc decision during an outage. Set clear conditions for when an alert becomes an incident, and connect those triggers to structured escalation plans and on-call schedules so the right people are notified immediately. Assign explicit roles and responsibilities, including who is responsible, accountable, consulted, and informed, so responders do not waste time figuring out ownership. Where appropriate, pre-approve common recovery actions to reduce delays when an incident needs quick intervention. Keep your alerts tied to actionable response steps and an up-to-date runbook so any on-call engineer can follow the same path under pressure.

**Why it matters:** Clear escalation paths reduce confusion, improve coordination, and shorten mean time to resolution. They also help ensure the right experts are engaged early, which limits blast radius and keeps service impact contained.
