## Tip of the day [Sunday, September 6, 2026]

### Centralize logs in a tamper-resistant archive

Aggregate security-related logs from your AWS services and applications into a dedicated log archive account instead of leaving them scattered across workloads. Include high-value sources like CloudTrail, WAF, load balancer logs, Route 53 logs, and any application or operating system logs that support investigations. Centralizing these records gives you one place to apply retention, lifecycle, and access controls, and it simplifies cross-account analysis and alerting. It also reduces the risk of log tampering if a workload account is compromised. If you use a standard schema or a service such as Security Lake, you make correlation and downstream detection much easier.

**Why it matters:** Centralized security logging improves your ability to detect, investigate, and prove what happened during an incident. It also supports compliance and audit needs by keeping logs consistently retained and easier to search, enrich, and protect.
