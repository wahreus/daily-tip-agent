## Tip of the day [Sunday, September 13, 2026]

### Choose architecture by measuring trade-offs, not by habit

For architecture selection, start with the workload’s outcomes and constraints, then compare candidate designs with data instead of assumptions. The Well-Architected guidance emphasizes that the optimal solution varies by workload and that many effective architectures combine multiple approaches. Use proof-of-concepts, benchmarking, and provider guidance to validate performance, cost, and customer impact before standardizing on a pattern. When evaluating options, explicitly test the trade-offs between reliability, performance, complexity, and cost, since improving two often means sacrificing the third. Revisit the decision regularly as cloud services evolve and new options may better fit the same workload. Grounding the choice in measured results helps you pick the simplest design that still meets your needs.

**Why it matters:** Better architecture selection reduces the risk of overengineering, hidden cost, and performance surprises after launch. It also helps teams adapt as requirements change, instead of locking into an approach that looked good on paper but fails under real-world conditions.
