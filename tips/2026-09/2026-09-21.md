## Tip of the day [Mon, September 21, 2026]

### Right-size from real utilization, not guesswork

Review your compute and storage usage on a regular schedule and look for resources that stay idle or consistently underutilized. For stable workloads, use rightsizing tools like AWS Compute Optimizer and check whether CPU and memory usage stay well below what the instance can handle over a multi-week period. For ephemeral workloads, rely more on utilization metrics and workload patterns so you can spot short-lived waste quickly. Don’t stop at instance size changes: remove unused components entirely, and consider consolidating small, underused workloads onto fewer, appropriately sized resources. Revisit recommendations over time because workload demand, instance families, and service options change.

**Why it matters:** Right-sizing helps you avoid paying for capacity you do not use while also reducing the risk of overloading undersized resources. It improves both cost efficiency and operational stability by matching resources more closely to real demand.
