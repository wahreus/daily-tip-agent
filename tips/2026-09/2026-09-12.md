## Tip of the day [Saturday, September 12, 2026]

### Build a closed-loop improvement cycle

Treat continuous operational improvement as a repeatable loop: collect signals from observability, incident reviews, and customer feedback, then convert them into prioritized actions. Make post-incident analysis mandatory for customer-impacting events, and capture contributing factors plus preventative actions so recurrence is less likely. Review operational items regularly in a structured problem-management process, not just when something breaks. Use scripted responses and automation for well-understood issues so your team can spend more time on higher-value improvements. Keep the loop cross-functional by involving operations, development, and business stakeholders when deciding what to fix next.

**Why it matters:** Feedback loops are what turn day-to-day operations into lasting resilience and efficiency gains. Without them, teams tend to repeat the same incidents and miss opportunities to improve runbooks, tooling, and architecture.
