## Tip of the day [Tuesday, September 1, 2026]

### Put security ownership in the team, not just the security org

Embed security decision-making in each workload team so developers and builders can make secure choices early, while the security team focuses on review and guidance. Give each application, workload, platform, and infrastructure component a clearly identified owner, and make sure security-related processes and procedures also have owners. For incident response, define who is responsible, accountable, consulted, and informed so teams can move quickly without confusion. Build a simple RACI for security events and practice it with application owners and SMEs before an incident happens. Avoid the anti-pattern of routing every security decision through a central security team, because that slows delivery and pushes risk discovery too late.

**Why it matters:** Clear ownership shortens decision time and improves accountability when security issues arise. It also helps teams catch and fix risks earlier in the development cycle, which leads to safer workloads and smoother operations.
