## Tip of the day [Wednesday, September 16, 2026]

### Test recovery against real RTO and RPO targets

Don’t treat backups as proof of recoverability; run periodic recovery tests that restore data and verify the application can actually read and use it. Define clear RTO and RPO targets first, then measure whether your restore process brings the workload back within those limits. Test more than just the backup file itself: confirm the restored resource is intact, accessible, and free of corruption. Automate these recovery exercises where possible so the process is repeatable, observable, and easy to invoke during an incident. If your tests show the restore takes too long or data loss exceeds your objective, revise the backup, replication, or failover design rather than assuming it will work in production.

**Why it matters:** Recovery objectives only matter if you can meet them under pressure. Regular testing exposes hidden gaps in backup integrity, restore automation, and incident readiness before a real outage does.
