## Tip of the day [Tue, September 22, 2026]

### Automate decommissioning of idle cloud resources

Build a regular cleanup process that identifies unused or underutilized resources and retires them before they accumulate cost and risk. For stable workloads, use rightsizing tools like AWS Compute Optimizer on a recurring basis to find idle or oversized components. For ephemeral workloads, rely on utilization metrics to spot resources that are no longer serving demand. Include dependent assets in the cleanup, such as EBS volumes, snapshots, Elastic IPs, AMIs, and container images, so you do not leave behind orphaned spend. When possible, automate deletions with services like AWS Config, Systems Manager, or lifecycle policies instead of depending on manual reviews. Always verify that a resource is truly unused before removing it.

**Why it matters:** Unused resources quietly consume budget, create operational clutter, and can expand your attack surface. Regular decommissioning helps align infrastructure with actual demand and keeps your environment easier to manage.
