## Tip of the day [Monday, September 14, 2026]

### Make architecture choices with benchmarks, not assumptions

For workload architecture selection, start by understanding the available cloud services and the patterns they support, then test the options that fit your requirements. Use proof-of-concepts and benchmarking to compare candidate designs under realistic load, because the “best” solution often combines multiple approaches. Factor cost into the decision early, and explicitly document the trade-offs between performance, complexity, and efficiency. If a design improves latency or availability, verify whether the added operational overhead or spend is justified for the customer outcome. Revisit the choice regularly as AWS services evolve and your workload needs change.

**Why it matters:** Architecture selection affects every pillar, so a guess can lock you into poor performance or unnecessary cost. A data-driven evaluation helps you choose a design that balances customer experience, operational effort, and cloud efficiency.
