## Tip of the day [Tuesday, September 29, 2026]

### Build playbooks with clear phases and owners

Create incident response playbooks for the scenarios you expect most, such as credential compromise, DoS, or ransomware. Each playbook should start with the incident goal, required logs and detection signals, and a clear list of who is responsible for each action. Structure the response around the full lifecycle: detect, analyze, contain, eradicate, and recover. Keep the playbook centrally stored, easy for the team to find, and linked from alerts so responders can jump straight to the right guidance. As you mature, automate the low-risk discovery steps and pair each playbook with a runbook for known root causes. Review and update the playbooks regularly so they stay aligned with your environment and tooling.

**Why it matters:** Clear playbooks reduce guesswork during high-stress incidents and lower the chance of human error. They also make response faster and more consistent, which improves mean time to detect and resolve issues.
