## Tip of the day [Tuesday, September 8, 2026]

### Map keys to workload and data classification

Use AWS KMS keys that are scoped to the workload using them, and prefer keeping customer managed keys in the same account as that workload. When you have different data classes or environments, avoid reusing one key everywhere; map keys to the sensitivity and usage of the data so access can be controlled more precisely. Choose AWS managed keys when you want simplicity, and customer managed keys when you need tighter control over policies, sovereignty, or operational ownership. Review KMS key policies and grants carefully, because they are the main controls that determine who can decrypt data or use services on your behalf. Enable monitoring in CloudTrail and watch for unusual key usage or key deletion events, since those can indicate accidental exposure or malicious activity.

**Why it matters:** Proper key scoping reduces the blast radius if a key policy is misconfigured or credentials are compromised. It also helps prevent accidental access across environments and makes it easier to enforce least privilege and comply with data protection requirements.
