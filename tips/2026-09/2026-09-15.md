## Tip of the day [Tue, September 15, 2026]

### Design for loose coupling and reusable components

Efficient software design starts by keeping dependencies loose so each component can evolve without forcing changes across the whole system. Use well-defined, versioned interfaces between services and prefer asynchronous interactions where immediate responses are not required. That approach reduces blast radius when a component fails and makes it easier to scale or replace parts independently. Reuse common functionality through shared, centrally managed packages or managed services instead of rebuilding the same logic in multiple places. Choosing serverless or managed components for repeated tasks can also cut operational overhead, letting teams focus more on product behavior than on infrastructure details.

**Why it matters:** Loosely coupled, reusable designs are easier to change safely, which improves delivery speed and lowers maintenance risk. They also help you scale more efficiently because you can optimize or replace one part of the system without redesigning everything else.
