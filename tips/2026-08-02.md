## Tip of the day [Sunday, August 2, 2026]

### Make metric reviews a scheduled maintenance task

Set a fixed cadence to review the metrics your workload collects, rather than only looking at them during incidents. During each review, check whether the current metrics still help explain problems, and add any missing signals that would have made past issues easier to detect or prevent. Prioritize business-outcome metrics as well as technical ones, so you can see whether performance changes are actually affecting users or revenue. If an alarm has been sitting in the same state for too long, treat that as a signal to tune the threshold, fix the underlying issue, or replace the alarm with something more actionable. Use the review to identify corrective actions like parameter tuning, bug fixes, or scaling changes, and then feed those actions back into your backlog.

**Why it matters:** Regular metric reviews keep monitoring from going stale and help you catch blind spots before they become incidents. They also improve the quality of your alerts, so engineers spend less time on noisy alarms and more time on issues that affect the business.
