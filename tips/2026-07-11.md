## Tip of the day [Saturday, July 11, 2026]

### Pre-stage your incident response path

Build your incident response process so the right logs, access, and automation are ready before an event happens. Use detailed logging and centralized alerts to make investigation and containment faster, and pre-provision the tooling you would need to isolate a workload or restore it to a known good state. Automate repeatable response steps with AWS APIs and runbooks so common alerts are handled consistently without burning out responders. Make sure the team regularly practices game days, so roles, escalation paths, and technical recovery steps are actually usable under pressure. Keep humans focused on unusual or high-risk incidents, while automation handles the routine ones.

**Why it matters:** Good incident response depends as much on preparation as detection. When access, logs, and response automation are already in place, you can reduce time to contain, recover faster, and avoid mistakes caused by alert fatigue.
