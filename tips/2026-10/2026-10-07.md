## Tip of the day [Wednesday, October 7, 2026]

### Match compute style to workload shape

Choose your compute option based on how the workload actually behaves, not just on what is familiar. For steady, control-heavy applications, a virtual server model may be the best fit; for event-driven code, a function-based model can avoid managing idle capacity; and for containerized services, a container platform can balance portability with operational control. Avoid over-provisioning to cover peak demand when the workload can scale dynamically or be split across multiple compute types. Use the available instance families, sizes, and features to align with CPU, memory, storage, or accelerator needs instead of treating all compute as interchangeable. Revisit the choice as your application design and traffic patterns change so the architecture stays efficient.

**Why it matters:** Picking the wrong compute model can hurt performance efficiency and waste cost. A workload-aware selection helps you right-size resources, scale more effectively, and use the strengths of each compute option where they fit best.
