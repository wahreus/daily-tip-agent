## Tip of the day [Sunday, October 4, 2026]

### Enforce MFA at the identity source

Require multi-factor authentication for every human identity that can access AWS, and make MFA part of your centralized identity provider rather than an optional add-on. Use federated sign-in with a strong password policy, and prefer context-aware or always-on MFA settings where your identity source supports them. For AWS IAM Identity Center or a connected directory, let users register their own MFA devices to reduce friction and improve adoption. Monitor MFA configuration continuously with AWS Config Rules or AWS Security Hub so missing enrollment is detected quickly. Avoid shared credentials, since individual identities make MFA enforcement and auditability much stronger.

**Why it matters:** MFA significantly reduces the chance that stolen or guessed credentials lead to unauthorized access. Enforcing it centrally also makes compliance easier and gives you a single place to monitor, audit, and improve your sign-in controls.
