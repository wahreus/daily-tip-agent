## Tip of the day [Monday, October 5, 2026]

### Right-size from real utilization, not guesswork

Review utilization metrics regularly and use AWS rightsizing tools like Compute Optimizer, Cost Explorer, and Trusted Advisor to find idle or underutilized resources. For compute, look at CPU and memory trends over a meaningful window so you can spot instances that consistently run below needed capacity. For stable workloads, make rightsizing checks part of a recurring review instead of a one-time cleanup. For storage, use visibility tools such as S3 Storage Lens to identify buckets or data patterns that are costing more than they should. When possible, replace oversized resources with smaller ones, or consolidate underused components so the workload still meets performance needs. Pair rightsizing with Auto Scaling so capacity can expand and contract with demand instead of staying permanently overprovisioned.

**Why it matters:** Right-sizing helps you avoid paying for unused capacity while keeping performance aligned with actual workload demand. It also reduces waste, improves operational efficiency, and gives you a clearer view of where architecture changes can deliver the biggest savings.
