## Tip of the day [Tuesday, October 6, 2026]

### Pre-stage your incident response path

Treat incident response as an architecture requirement, not something to invent during a crisis. Make sure your team has detailed logging enabled, automated event handling where possible, and pre-provisioned tooling that can be used to isolate, investigate, and restore affected systems quickly. Define roles, responsibilities, and escalation paths ahead of time, and include at least two contact channels per responder so people are reachable during an incident. Practice the full response flow with game days so your team can verify that the runbooks, access, and automation actually work under pressure. Keep “break glass” and temporary elevated access processes reviewed and tested so emergency access is available without becoming a standing risk.

**Why it matters:** When a security event happens, speed and clarity determine how much damage you can contain. Pre-built access, logging, and automation reduce manual steps and help avoid alert fatigue, while rehearsed roles and contacts prevent confusion during high-stress response.
