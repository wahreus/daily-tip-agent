## Tip of the day [Thursday, October 8, 2026]

### Centralize security logs in a dedicated archive account

Collect security-related logs from all workloads and AWS services into a standardized, separate log archive account instead of leaving them scattered across teams and environments. Include sources such as CloudTrail, WAF, load balancer logs, Route 53 logs, and any application or OS logs that help explain security events. This gives security teams one place to correlate activity, reduces the complexity of cross-account investigations, and makes it easier to connect the data to SIEM or other analysis tools. Use fine-grained cross-account access so analysts can query what they need without exposing raw logs broadly. Pair the central repository with lifecycle and retention policies so security data stays available for investigations and compliance without creating unnecessary storage cost.

**Why it matters:** Centralized logging improves detection speed because analysts can correlate events across systems without chasing logs through many accounts and tools. It also helps protect evidence from tampering if a workload account is compromised, while making retention and access control easier to govern.
