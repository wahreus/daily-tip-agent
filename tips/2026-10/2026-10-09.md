## Tip of the day [Friday, October 9, 2026]

### Layer network defenses by exposure

Design your network in layers so that only the components that truly need internet access are exposed, while internal services such as databases stay on private paths. Use security groups, route tables, and firewalls together to control traffic between layers instead of relying on a single perimeter rule set. Add inspection points between tiers so traffic can be checked against expected patterns, not just ports and protocols. For public web entry points, place protection at the edge with services like AWS WAF and AWS Shield, and keep deeper inspection for traffic moving toward sensitive data. Automate these controls where possible so new workloads inherit the same network protections without manual drift.

**Why it matters:** Layered network protection reduces the blast radius of a compromise and makes unauthorized movement through your environment harder. It also improves detection and response because traffic is reviewed closer to the point where it should be allowed or denied.
