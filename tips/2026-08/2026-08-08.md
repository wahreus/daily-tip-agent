## Tip of the day [Saturday, August 8, 2026]

### Right-size from real utilization, not guesswork

Review compute usage regularly and use both CPU and memory metrics to spot idle or underutilized resources. For stable workloads, check rightsizing tools like AWS Compute Optimizer on a routine basis; for ephemeral workloads, rely on utilization trends over time to find the same opportunities. A practical threshold from the guidance is to look for instances whose maximum CPU and memory usage stay below about 40% over a four-week period. Once you identify those resources, either move to a smaller instance type, consolidate workloads, or remove components that are no longer needed. Keep an eye on demand patterns too, so you can pair right-sizing with Auto Scaling and avoid paying for capacity that sits unused.

**Why it matters:** Right-sizing helps you avoid both over-provisioning and performance issues caused by poor fit between workload demand and resource size. It also improves cost efficiency by ensuring you only pay for the capacity you actually need.
