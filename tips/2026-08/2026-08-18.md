## Tip of the day [Tue, August 18, 2026]

### Move workforce access to federated identities

Centralize workforce authentication in a single identity provider or AWS IAM Identity Center, then federate access into AWS accounts instead of relying on IAM users and groups. Keep user group membership and assigned permissions tied to employee lifecycle events, so role changes and departures automatically update access. Use single sign-on for console and CLI access, and enforce MFA on the identity provider and the AWS root user. Remove IAM users, groups, and long-lived access keys that are no longer needed, and consider an SCP that prevents new IAM users and groups from being created. For multi-account environments, federate to the organization rather than managing separate local identities per account.

**Why it matters:** Centralized identity management reduces credential sprawl and makes access changes faster and more consistent. It also lowers the risk from stale accounts and long-lived credentials, while improving auditability and making least-privilege enforcement easier across accounts.
