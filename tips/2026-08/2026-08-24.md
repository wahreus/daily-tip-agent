## Tip of the day [Monday, August 24, 2026]

### Automate cleanup of idle capacity

Build a recurring check for unused or underutilized resources, then automate the cleanup of anything that no longer adds value. For steady-state workloads, review rightsizing recommendations from tools like AWS Compute Optimizer on a regular cadence. For ephemeral workloads, use utilization metrics such as CPU, memory, and network throughput to spot idle instances, volumes, and other stranded assets. Where possible, use EC2 Auto Scaling or Application Auto Scaling to terminate or scale in non-critical capacity automatically instead of waiting for manual intervention. Also remember to retire associated resources like unused ECR images and unattached EBS volumes so hidden waste does not linger. A small but consistent cleanup loop is usually more effective than occasional large-scale remediation.

**Why it matters:** Idle resources quietly increase cost without improving performance or reliability. Automating detection and decommissioning helps you keep the environment lean, reduce waste, and maintain better utilization over time.
