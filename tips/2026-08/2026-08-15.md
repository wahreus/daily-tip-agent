## Tip of the day [Saturday, August 15, 2026]

### Track sustainability per unit of work

Define sustainability KPIs that relate resource use and emissions to a unit of business output, not just raw infrastructure metrics. Start by measuring compute utilization, memory, disk I/O, and network activity alongside your workload’s actual productive output, then compare them over time to see whether efficiency is improving. Include carbon accounting boundaries so you know which emissions sources belong in scope, and align the metrics with goals from IT, finance, and sustainability teams. Keep the measurements in one monitoring platform with clear retention policies so you can trend them reliably and avoid metric sprawl. Use the results to identify underutilized resources, right-size workloads, and prioritize managed services where they reduce shared infrastructure impact.

**Why it matters:** Raw usage numbers alone do not show whether a workload is becoming more sustainable. Per-unit metrics make it easier to prove progress against carbon reduction and resource optimization goals, and they help engineering teams focus on changes that actually lower environmental impact.
