## Tip of the day [Sunday, August 23, 2026]

### Measure sustainability per unit of work

Define sustainability KPIs that connect environmental impact to business output, not just total resource consumption. Measure resources and emissions per unit of work, such as per transaction, per build, per request, or per gigabyte processed, so you can see whether efficiency is improving as usage grows. Set clear goals with stakeholders from engineering, finance, and sustainability to align the metrics with broader business targets. Include the full workload impact in your thinking, not only infrastructure usage, and account for how cloud region choice and managed services affect efficiency. Review these metrics regularly so you can compare workloads, spot regressions, and prioritize optimization efforts where they will have the most effect.

**Why it matters:** Sustainability metrics are most useful when they show whether a workload is delivering more value with less environmental impact. Tracking per-unit efficiency helps teams make better architecture decisions and proves progress toward carbon and resource optimization goals.
