## Tip of the day [Saturday, August 29, 2026]

### Favor managed services to lower your footprint

When designing for sustainability, prefer managed services over self-managed infrastructure whenever possible. Managed services let AWS handle a larger share of hardware utilization and operational efficiency, which helps spread the sustainability impact across many tenants instead of carrying it all on your own stack. In practice, this can reduce excess capacity from underutilized servers and free your team from routine platform maintenance. Use the time and operational savings to simplify workloads further and remove resources that are no longer needed. For variable compute needs, pair managed services with automation so you only keep capacity running for as long as it is actually required.

**Why it matters:** Sustainability gains often come from reducing idle or duplicated infrastructure, not just from smaller instances. Managed services can improve efficiency while also making it easier to focus engineering effort on higher-value optimizations.
