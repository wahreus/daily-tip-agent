## Tip of the day [Wednesday, August 5, 2026]

### Standardize incident playbooks before the outage happens

Build incident response playbooks for the scenarios you expect most, such as credential compromise, DoS, or a known alert pattern. Each playbook should clearly state the incident goal, required logs and detection signals, who to notify, and the responsibilities of each stakeholder. Include concrete response steps for the full lifecycle: detect, analyze, contain, eradicate, and recover. Store the playbooks centrally, link them from alerts, and keep companion runbooks for known root causes so responders can move from investigation to remediation without improvising. Start with low-risk incidents and gradually automate the discovery steps as your team matures.

**Why it matters:** Well-structured playbooks reduce human error and remove guesswork during stressful incidents. They also shorten mean time to detect and resolve by giving engineers a repeatable path to follow, and they make it easier to test and improve your response process over time.
