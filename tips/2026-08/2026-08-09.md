## Tip of the day [Sunday, August 9, 2026]

### Make security ownership explicit in every workload team

Assign clear security decision-making responsibility inside each workload team instead of routing every choice through a central security group. Train builders to recognize security requirements early, and pair them with security specialists where needed so decisions can be made sooner in the delivery cycle. Document who owns each application, workload, platform, and supporting process, and make those owners easy to find during reviews or incidents. Define where the workload team can decide independently and where security review or approval is still required. This reduces delay, improves accountability, and helps teams respond faster when risks or exceptions appear.

**Why it matters:** When security ownership sits inside the team that builds and runs the service, security is addressed earlier and more consistently. Clear ownership also prevents gaps where everyone assumes someone else is responsible, which lowers operational risk and speeds up escalation when action is needed.
