## Tip of the day [Wed, August 19, 2026]

### Secure backups like a separate security zone

Treat backup protection as more than just retention: encrypt backups, lock down access with least privilege, and avoid giving the backup system the same permissions as production. For critical data, use logical isolation such as a separate AWS Backup vault, and consider immutable or air-gapped storage to reduce the risk of deletion or tampering. Backups should also be restored and tested regularly so you know the data is intact and the recovery process works before an incident forces you to find out. If you replicate backups across Regions, make sure encryption is configured on both the source and destination side. For high-risk recovery workflows, add approval controls so a single compromised identity cannot trigger a malicious restore.

**Why it matters:** Backup protection fails when attackers can encrypt, delete, or restore data through the same trust path as production. Strong isolation, encryption, and restore testing improve resilience against ransomware and accidental loss while making recovery more trustworthy.
