## Tip of the day [Thursday, August 20, 2026]

### Routinely decommission what you no longer use

Set up a recurring review for idle or underutilized resources, and pair it with automated cleanup wherever possible. For stable workloads, use rightsizing tools such as AWS Compute Optimizer on a regular schedule to find instances, volumes, or services that are consistently underused. For ephemeral or event-driven workloads, monitor utilization metrics so you can catch resources that are left running after the workload finishes. When you find low-value components, remove them or consolidate them with other resources instead of letting them quietly consume compute, storage, or network capacity. If the resource can be terminated safely, automate the decommissioning step with scaling or lifecycle controls so the cleanup happens consistently.

**Why it matters:** Idle resources create waste and drive up cost without improving performance. Regular cleanup and automation help you keep utilization high, reduce operational drift, and avoid paying for capacity you no longer need.
