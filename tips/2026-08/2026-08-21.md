## Tip of the day [Friday, August 21, 2026]

### Centralize security logs in a dedicated archive account

Aggregate security-relevant logs from across your workloads and AWS services into a separate Log Archive account instead of leaving them scattered across individual accounts and teams. Include sources like CloudTrail, WAF, Elastic Load Balancing, Route 53, and other service or application logs that support investigation and detection. Standardizing these logs in one place reduces ETL and access-management overhead while giving you a single integration point for analytics and response tooling. It also helps protect logs from tampering if a workload account is compromised, because the archive is isolated with cross-account controls. For long-term retention, use storage and lifecycle policies that fit your investigation and compliance needs.

**Why it matters:** Centralized logging makes it much easier to detect patterns, correlate events, and respond quickly during an incident. A separate archive also improves trust in the logs themselves by reducing the chance that an attacker can alter or delete evidence.
