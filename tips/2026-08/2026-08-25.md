## Tip of the day [Tuesday, August 25, 2026]

### Right-size the hardware to the workload

Start by listing the workload’s concrete requirements: CPU cores, memory, storage type and capacity, network bandwidth, and any GPU or accelerator needs. Then match those needs to an EC2 instance family and size instead of defaulting to a general-purpose option. For spiky workloads, consider burstable instances so you only pay for sustained capacity when you actually need it. If the workload is stateless and fault tolerant, Spot Instances can improve utilization and reduce waste. For machine learning or other specialized workloads, choose purpose-built hardware such as Inferentia, Trainium, or accelerated instances rather than forcing the job onto standard CPU-based compute. Revisit the choice periodically with tools like Compute Optimizer or rightsizing recommendations so the hardware stays aligned as usage changes.

**Why it matters:** Picking the right hardware improves performance efficiency and avoids paying for capacity you do not need. It also reduces wasted energy and helps your platform stay adaptable as workload patterns evolve.
