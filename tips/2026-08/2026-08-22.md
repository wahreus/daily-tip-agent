## Tip of the day [Sat, August 22, 2026]

### Turn repeatable ops into automated runbooks

Document routine operational tasks as clear runbooks, then automate the short, frequently used ones first. Keep the runbook in a central place, include the intended outcome, required permissions, error handling, and escalation steps, and make sure someone else on the team can successfully execute it. As your practice matures, convert those runbooks into automations such as AWS Systems Manager Automation so they can run consistently in response to events. This reduces manual drift, speeds up routine operations, and makes it easier to respond the same way every time. For non-routine incidents, keep playbooks focused on investigation and response, while runbooks handle the repeatable execution steps.  

**Why it matters:** Operations as code lowers human error by making the procedure explicit, testable, and repeatable. It also improves resilience because teams can execute the same approved process under pressure, even when the original author is unavailable.
