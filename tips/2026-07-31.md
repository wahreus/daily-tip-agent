## Tip of the day [Friday, July 31, 2026]

### Right-size with feedback, not guesswork

Build a regular review loop around utilization metrics so you can see where compute is consistently over- or under-provisioned. Use tools like AWS Compute Optimizer, Cost Explorer, and Trusted Advisor to spot idle, unused, or underutilized resources before they become ongoing waste. For stable workloads, check right-sizing recommendations at fixed intervals; for ephemeral workloads, rely more on live utilization metrics and recent demand patterns. Pair those findings with Auto Scaling or other automatic feedback mechanisms so capacity rises and falls with real workload demand. When appropriate, refactor or consolidate small, underused components instead of leaving them spread across separate instances.  

**Why it matters:** Right-sizing improves both cost efficiency and performance by reducing waste without starving workloads. It also creates a repeatable operating practice that helps you adapt as demand, instance types, and service options change over time.
