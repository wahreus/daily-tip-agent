## Tip of the day [Sunday, July 12, 2026]

### Use managed services to cut operational overhead

Review workload components that are still carrying the burden of patching, upgrades, backups, or OS maintenance and replace them with managed services where it makes sense. Managed services can reduce license costs, lower the operational and administrative effort your team spends on upkeep, and often deliver a lower cost per transaction at cloud scale. Start with high-effort services like databases, analytics, and caching, then compare the total cost of ownership against the managed alternative instead of looking only at instance pricing. This approach lets you keep the core architecture intact while reducing the day-to-day work required to run it. It also frees engineering time for technical debt reduction and feature delivery.

**Why it matters:** Cost reduction is not just about cheaper infrastructure; it is also about shrinking the hidden cost of managing it. Using managed services can improve both financial efficiency and team productivity, which compounds savings over time.
