## Tip of the day [Saturday, August 1, 2026]

### Use utilization data to right-size regularly

Review CPU and memory metrics for your compute fleet on a regular schedule, rather than only when costs spike. For stable workloads, use AWS Compute Optimizer and Cost Explorer to find idle, unused, or underutilized instances and compare their recommendations against your performance requirements. Look for instances that stay below roughly 40% CPU and memory usage over a multi-week window, since those are strong candidates for downsizing or consolidation. For ephemeral workloads, focus on CloudWatch metrics to spot idle capacity and remove it sooner. Don’t stop at compute: also check for unused storage and associated assets like EBS volumes or ECR images that can be retired. Make right-sizing part of an ongoing optimization loop so you can keep performance steady while reducing waste.

**Why it matters:** Right-sizing helps you avoid paying for capacity you are not using while still meeting workload demand. It also improves operational efficiency by keeping resource choices aligned with real workload behavior instead of assumptions.
